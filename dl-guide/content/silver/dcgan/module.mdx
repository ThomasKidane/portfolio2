# DCGAN

## Historical question and bottleneck

[Unsupervised Representation Learning with Deep Convolutional Generative Adversarial Networks](https://arxiv.org/abs/1511.06434) (Radford et al., 2015) is the primary reference. The original question motivating this module is how to train MNIST convolutional generator and discriminator; audit classifier confidence and class diversity. The practical bottleneck for this exercise is explicit: Report frozen-classifier confidence and per-class diversity separately; no single proxy PASS. This is a reduced teaching adaptation, not a claim that the paper used our dataset or compute budget.

## Mechanism and equations

$$
\begin{aligned}
\min_G\max_D\ \mathbb E_{x\sim p_{\mathrm{data}}}\log D(x)+\mathbb E_{z\sim p_z}\log(1-D(G(z))),\\ L_G^{\mathrm{NS}}=-\mathbb E_{z\sim p_z}\log D(G(z))
\end{aligned}
$$

Here G generates from noise z, D estimates real-data probability, and x is a real sample. The second line is the non-saturating generator loss used instead of minimizing the saturating minimax term; this alone does not specify the DCGAN architecture.

Derive each tensor's dimensions before implementing the equation. State the normalization axis, numerical stabilizer and train/evaluation behavior. Distinguish a mathematical mechanism from the full original training system.

## Implementation notes

Input/task: **mnist**. Output: **Noise -> images; discriminator returns real/fake logits**. Optimize or report **generation_quality** in the **maximize** direction. Report frozen-classifier confidence and per-class diversity separately; no single proxy PASS.

This is a draft skeleton: reference training and task-specific tests are not implemented. The explicit stub must not be mistaken for a finished model.

## Controlled experiment

Remove generator batch normalization at fixed training updates. Keep split, seed, optimizer schedule and training budget fixed unless that factor is the experimental variable. Report raw quality, wall time and parameter count, not an unsupported superiority claim.

## Pitfalls and checks

- Report frozen-classifier confidence and per-class diversity separately; no single proxy PASS.
- Never fit statistics on validation labels or mix held-out examples into training.
- Check shape and finite outputs before interpreting quality; an unrelated baseline cannot satisfy the intended mechanism.
- Draft targets and safety limits have not been GPU-calibrated. No pass threshold is advertised.
