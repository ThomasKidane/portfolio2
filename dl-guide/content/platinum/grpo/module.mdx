# Group-relative policy optimization

## Historical question and bottleneck

[DeepSeekMath: Pushing the Limits of Mathematical Reasoning in Open Language Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.03300) (Shao et al., 2024) is the primary reference. The original question motivating this module is how to train on generated verifiable arithmetic using group-normalized rewards without a value network. The practical bottleneck for this exercise is explicit: Exact answer verifier and train/eval operand split; handle zero group reward variance. This is a reduced teaching adaptation, not a claim that the paper used our dataset or compute budget.

## Mechanism and equations

$$
\begin{aligned}
\hat A_i=\frac{R_i-\bar R}{s_R+\delta},\quad r_{it}=\frac{\pi_\theta(o_{it}\mid x,o_{i,1:t-1})}{\pi_{\mathrm{old}}(o_{it}\mid x,o_{i,1:t-1})},\\ J=\mathbb E\frac1G\sum_{i=1}^G\frac1{T_i}\sum_{t=1}^{T_i}\left[\min(r_{it}\hat A_i,\operatorname{clip}(r_{it},1-\epsilon,1+\epsilon)\hat A_i)-\beta k_{it}\right],\\ u_{it}=\frac{\pi_{\mathrm{ref}}(o_{it}\mid x,o_{i,1:t-1})}{\pi_\theta(o_{it}\mid x,o_{i,1:t-1})},\quad k_{it}=u_{it}-\log u_{it}-1
\end{aligned}
$$

Here G completions o share prompt x, R are rewards, bar-R/s-R group mean/standard deviation, delta a stabilizer, T-i completion lengths, epsilon clip width, and beta reference-penalty weight. Maximize J with fixed advantages; old is the sampling policy and ref a frozen reference. The shown sampled KL surrogate and length averaging specify one convention, not every GRPO variant.

Derive each tensor's dimensions before implementing the equation. State the normalization axis, numerical stabilizer and train/evaluation behavior. Distinguish a mathematical mechanism from the full original training system.

## Implementation notes

Input/task: **generated-arithmetic**. Output: **Arithmetic prompt -> sampled answer and policy log probabilities**. Optimize or report **verified_accuracy** in the **maximize** direction. Exact answer verifier and train/eval operand split; handle zero group reward variance.

This is a draft skeleton: reference training and task-specific tests are not implemented. The explicit stub must not be mistaken for a finished model.

## Controlled experiment

Compare group-relative and unnormalized rewards with the same sampled completions. Keep split, seed, optimizer schedule and training budget fixed unless that factor is the experimental variable. Report raw quality, wall time and parameter count, not an unsupported superiority claim.

## Pitfalls and checks

- Exact answer verifier and train/eval operand split; handle zero group reward variance.
- Never fit statistics on validation labels or mix held-out examples into training.
- Check shape and finite outputs before interpreting quality; an unrelated baseline cannot satisfy the intended mechanism.
