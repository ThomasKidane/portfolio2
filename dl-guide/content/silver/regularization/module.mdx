# Dropout and weight decay

## Historical question and bottleneck

[Dropout: A Simple Way to Prevent Neural Networks from Overfitting](https://jmlr.org/papers/v15/srivastava14a.html) (Srivastava et al., 2014) is the primary reference. The original question motivating this module is how to compare dropout and decoupled decay on a fixed 5000-example CIFAR-10 training subset. The practical bottleneck for this exercise is explicit: Exactly 5000 training images; fixed validation set; compare dropout and decay. This is a reduced teaching adaptation, not a claim that the paper used our dataset or compute budget.

## Mechanism and equations

$$
\begin{aligned}
L_{\mathrm{total}}=L_{\mathrm{data}}+\frac\lambda2\lVert w\rVert_2^2,\\ w_{t+1}=(1-\eta\lambda)w_t-\eta u_t
\end{aligned}
$$

Here w is the weight vector, lambda the penalty/decay coefficient, eta the learning rate, and u the optimizer's data-gradient update direction. The first line is coupled L2 regularization; the second is decoupled decay. They agree for plain SGD but generally not adaptive optimizers.

Derive each tensor's dimensions before implementing the equation. State the normalization axis, numerical stabilizer and train/evaluation behavior. Distinguish a mathematical mechanism from the full original training system.

## Implementation notes

Input/task: **cifar10**. Output: **[N,3,32,32] -> [N,10] logits**. Optimize or report **accuracy** in the **maximize** direction. Exactly 5000 training images; fixed validation set; compare dropout and decay.

This is a draft skeleton: reference training and task-specific tests are not implemented. The explicit stub must not be mistaken for a finished model.

## Controlled experiment

Run no-regularization dropout-only decay-only and combined controls. Keep split, seed, optimizer schedule and training budget fixed unless that factor is the experimental variable. Report raw quality, wall time and parameter count, not an unsupported superiority claim.

## Pitfalls and checks

- Exactly 5000 training images; fixed validation set; compare dropout and decay.
- Never fit statistics on validation labels or mix held-out examples into training.
- Check shape and finite outputs before interpreting quality; an unrelated baseline cannot satisfy the intended mechanism.
