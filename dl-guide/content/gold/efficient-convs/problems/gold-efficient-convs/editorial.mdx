# MobileNet and EfficientNet: derivation and implementation notes

## The mechanism

$$
\begin{aligned}
C_{\mathrm{dense}}=HWk^2C_{\mathrm{in}}C_{\mathrm{out}},\\ C_{\mathrm{sep}}=HW(k^2C_{\mathrm{in}}+C_{\mathrm{in}}C_{\mathrm{out}})
\end{aligned}
$$

Here H/W are output height/width, k kernel side, and C-in/C-out channel counts. C counts multiply-accumulates, excluding biases and nonlinearities, for a dense convolution versus depthwise then pointwise convolution with depth multiplier one.
