# Vision Transformer

## Historical question and bottleneck

[An Image is Worth 16x16 Words: Transformers for Image Recognition at Scale](https://arxiv.org/abs/2010.11929) (Dosovitskiy et al., 2020) is the primary reference. The original question motivating this module is how to patchify CIFAR-10 and train a transformer classifier; aspirational accuracy 0.85 within 600 seconds. The practical bottleneck for this exercise is explicit: Patch size and position interpolation specified. This is a reduced teaching adaptation, not a claim that the paper used our dataset or compute budget.

## Mechanism and equations

`z_0=concat(class_token,flattened_patches*W_patch)+position_embeddings.`

Derive each tensor's dimensions before implementing the equation. State the normalization axis, numerical stabilizer and train/evaluation behavior. Distinguish a mathematical mechanism from the full original training system.

## Implementation notes

Input/task: **cifar10**. Output: **[N,3,32,32] -> [N,10] logits**. Optimize or report **accuracy** in the **maximize** direction. Patch size and position interpolation specified.

This is a draft skeleton: reference training and task-specific tests are not implemented. The explicit stub must not be mistaken for a finished model.

## Controlled experiment

Compare class-token and mean-pooled heads with identical patch encodings. Keep split, seed, optimizer schedule and training budget fixed unless that factor is the experimental variable. Report raw quality, wall time and parameter count, not an unsupported superiority claim.

## Pitfalls and checks

- Patch size and position interpolation specified.
- Never fit statistics on validation labels or mix held-out examples into training.
- Check shape and finite outputs before interpreting quality; an unrelated baseline cannot satisfy the intended mechanism.
- Draft targets and safety limits have not been GPU-calibrated. No pass threshold is advertised.
