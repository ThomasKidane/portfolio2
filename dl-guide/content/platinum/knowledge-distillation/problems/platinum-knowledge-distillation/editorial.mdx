# Knowledge-distillation golf: derivation and implementation notes

## The mechanism

$$
\begin{aligned}
p_T=\operatorname{softmax}(z_T/T),\quad p_S=\operatorname{softmax}(z_S/T),\\ L=(1-\lambda)\operatorname{CE}(z_S,y)+\lambda T^2D_{\mathrm{KL}}(p_T\Vert p_S)
\end{aligned}
$$

Here z-T/z-S are teacher/student logits, T positive temperature, y labels, and lambda the mixture weight. CE uses untempered student logits; freeze the teacher and average both losses consistently.
