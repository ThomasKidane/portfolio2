# Low-rank adaptation

## Historical question and bottleneck

[LoRA: Low-Rank Adaptation of Large Language Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2106.09685) (Hu et al., 2021) is the primary reference. The original question motivating this module is how to fine-tune low-rank residual adapters with at most one percent trainable parameters and frozen base weights. The practical bottleneck for this exercise is explicit: Freeze base weights; low-rank delta merge agrees with unmerged output. This is a reduced teaching adaptation, not a claim that the paper used our dataset or compute budget.

## Mechanism and equations

$$
\begin{aligned}
W_{\mathrm{eff}}=W_0+\frac\alpha r BA,\\ A\in\mathbb R^{r\times d_{\mathrm{in}}},\quad B\in\mathbb R^{d_{\mathrm{out}}\times r},\\ f_{\mathrm{train}}=\frac{P_{\mathrm{trainable}}}{P_{\mathrm{total}}}
\end{aligned}
$$

Here W-zero is frozen, A/B are rank-r adapters, alpha their scale, d-in/d-out layer dimensions, and P parameter counts. State whether biases or other heads are trainable when reporting the fraction.

Derive each tensor's dimensions before implementing the equation. State the normalization axis, numerical stabilizer and train/evaluation behavior. Distinguish a mathematical mechanism from the full original training system.

## Implementation notes

Input/task: **licensed-adaptation-corpus**. Output: **Base-model input -> task logits; &lt;=1 percent trainable parameters**. Optimize or report **task_quality** in the **maximize** direction. Freeze base weights; low-rank delta merge agrees with unmerged output.

This is a draft skeleton: reference training and task-specific tests are not implemented. The explicit stub must not be mistaken for a finished model.

## Controlled experiment

Compare ranks at fixed trainable-parameter budget. Keep split, seed, optimizer schedule and training budget fixed unless that factor is the experimental variable. Report raw quality, wall time and parameter count, not an unsupported superiority claim.

## Pitfalls and checks

- Freeze base weights; low-rank delta merge agrees with unmerged output.
- Never fit statistics on validation labels or mix held-out examples into training.
- Check shape and finite outputs before interpreting quality; an unrelated baseline cannot satisfy the intended mechanism.
- Draft targets and safety limits have not been GPU-calibrated. No pass threshold is advertised.
