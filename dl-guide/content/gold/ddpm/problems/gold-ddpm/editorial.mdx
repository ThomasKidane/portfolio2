# Denoising diffusion probabilistic model: derivation and implementation notes

## The mechanism

$$
\begin{aligned}
\alpha_t=1-\beta_t,\quad\bar\alpha_t=\prod_{s=1}^t\alpha_s,\\ x_t=\sqrt{\bar\alpha_t}x_0+\sqrt{1-\bar\alpha_t}\varepsilon,\\ L_{\mathrm{simple}}=\mathbb E_{x_0,t,\varepsilon}\lVert\varepsilon_\theta(x_t,t)-\varepsilon\rVert_2^2
\end{aligned}
$$

Here beta is the noise variance schedule, x-zero clean data, epsilon standard Gaussian noise, t a sampled diffusion step, and epsilon-theta the noise predictor. The simplified noise loss omits the original variational weighting.
