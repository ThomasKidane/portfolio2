# REINFORCE with a baseline: derivation and implementation notes

## The mechanism

$$
\begin{aligned}
\nabla_\theta J=\mathbb E\!\left[\sum_t\nabla_\theta\log\pi_\theta(a_t\mid s_t)(G_t-b(s_t))\right],\\ G_t=\sum_{k=t}^{T-1}\gamma^{k-t}r_k
\end{aligned}
$$

Here pi is the policy, s/a states/actions, G reward-to-go, b an action-independent baseline, r reward, and T episode length. This objective uses undiscounted episodic returns when gamma equals one; a start-state discounted objective additionally weights each time term by gamma to the power t. Do not backpropagate through the sampled advantage in the policy update.

## Required ablation

## Failure modes

- Fitting on validation labels or allowing split overlap invalidates evaluation.
- Matching output shape alone does not establish that the intended mechanism was implemented.
- A small teaching model is not the original full-scale experiment.
