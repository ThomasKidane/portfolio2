# Pre-LN versus Post-LN stability: derivation and implementation notes

## The mechanism

$$
\begin{aligned}
x'_{\mathrm{pre}}=x+F(\operatorname{LN}(x)),\\ x'_{\mathrm{post}}=\operatorname{LN}(x+F(x))
\end{aligned}
$$

Here x is the residual stream, F an attention or feed-forward sublayer, and LN layer normalization over features. These equations isolate normalization placement for one residual sublayer.
