# Transformer

## Historical question and bottleneck

[Attention Is All You Need](https://arxiv.org/abs/1706.03762) (Vaswani et al., 2017) is the primary reference. The original question motivating this module is how to derive numerically stable scaled dot-product attention and explicit mask semantics. The practical bottleneck for this exercise is explicit: Compare SDPA values and gradients including non-square and fully masked cases. This is a reduced teaching adaptation, not a claim that the paper used our dataset or compute budget.

## Mechanism and equations

`Attention(Q,K,V)=softmax(QK^T/sqrt(d_k)+mask)V.`

Derive each tensor's dimensions before implementing the equation. State the normalization axis, numerical stabilizer and train/evaluation behavior. Distinguish a mathematical mechanism from the full original training system.

## Implementation notes

Input/task: **attention-equivalence**. Output: **forward(q,k,v,mask=None) -> attention output**. Optimize or report **attention_max_error** in the **minimize** direction. Compare SDPA values and gradients including non-square and fully masked cases.

An executable bounded reference and mechanism tests are available; work in the starter before opening the editorial.

## Controlled experiment

Remove the inverse-square-root key-width scale and compare scores and gradient norms. Keep split, seed, optimizer schedule and training budget fixed unless that factor is the experimental variable. Report raw quality, wall time and parameter count, not an unsupported superiority claim.

## Pitfalls and checks

- Compare SDPA values and gradients including non-square and fully masked cases.
- Never fit statistics on validation labels or mix held-out examples into training.
- Check shape and finite outputs before interpreting quality; an unrelated baseline cannot satisfy the intended mechanism.
- Draft targets and safety limits have not been GPU-calibrated. No pass threshold is advertised.
