# SimCLR

## Historical question and bottleneck

[A Simple Framework for Contrastive Learning of Visual Representations](https://arxiv.org/abs/2002.05709) (Chen et al., 2020) is the primary reference. The original question motivating this module is how to implement normalized temperature-scaled contrastive loss and a frozen CIFAR linear probe. The practical bottleneck for this exercise is explicit: Freeze encoder during probe; no label leakage into contrastive training. This is a reduced teaching adaptation, not a claim that the paper used our dataset or compute budget.

## Mechanism and equations

`L_i=-log exp(sim(z_i,z_pos)/tau)/sum_j exp(sim(z_i,z_j)/tau), excluding self.`

Derive each tensor's dimensions before implementing the equation. State the normalization axis, numerical stabilizer and train/evaluation behavior. Distinguish a mathematical mechanism from the full original training system.

## Implementation notes

Input/task: **cifar10**. Output: **Image -> normalized representation**. Optimize or report **linear_probe_accuracy** in the **maximize** direction. Freeze encoder during probe; no label leakage into contrastive training.

This is a draft skeleton: reference training and task-specific tests are not implemented. The explicit stub must not be mistaken for a finished model.

## Controlled experiment

Remove color augmentation while preserving batch size and probe schedule. Keep split, seed, optimizer schedule and training budget fixed unless that factor is the experimental variable. Report raw quality, wall time and parameter count, not an unsupported superiority claim.

## Pitfalls and checks

- Freeze encoder during probe; no label leakage into contrastive training.
- Never fit statistics on validation labels or mix held-out examples into training.
- Check shape and finite outputs before interpreting quality; an unrelated baseline cannot satisfy the intended mechanism.
- Draft targets and safety limits have not been GPU-calibrated. No pass threshold is advertised.
