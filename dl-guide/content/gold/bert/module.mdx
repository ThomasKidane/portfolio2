# BERT masked-language pretraining

## Historical question and bottleneck

[BERT: Pre-training of Deep Bidirectional Transformers for Language Understanding](https://arxiv.org/abs/1810.04805) (Devlin et al., 2018) is the primary reference. The original question motivating this module is how to implement masked-token pretraining and then fine-tune an SST-2 classifier without split leakage. The practical bottleneck for this exercise is explicit: Mask labels only; downstream SST-2 test labels excluded from selection. This is a reduced teaching adaptation, not a claim that the paper used our dataset or compute budget.

## Mechanism and equations

$$
\begin{aligned}
L_{\mathrm{MLM}}=-\sum_{i\in\mathcal M}\log p_\theta(x_i\mid\tilde x)
\end{aligned}
$$

Here x is the original token sequence, tilde-x its corrupted bidirectional input, M the selected prediction positions, and theta the model parameters. This is the masked-language objective only, not the full original BERT pretraining system.

Derive each tensor's dimensions before implementing the equation. State the normalization axis, numerical stabilizer and train/evaluation behavior. Distinguish a mathematical mechanism from the full original training system.

## Implementation notes

Input/task: **sst2-and-pretrain-corpus**. Output: **Masked tokens -> vocabulary logits; finetuned text -> binary logits**. Optimize or report **accuracy** in the **maximize** direction. Mask labels only; downstream SST-2 test labels excluded from selection.

This is a draft skeleton: reference training and task-specific tests are not implemented. The explicit stub must not be mistaken for a finished model.

## Controlled experiment

Compare pretrained and randomly initialized encoders at equal finetuning budget. Keep split, seed, optimizer schedule and training budget fixed unless that factor is the experimental variable. Report raw quality, wall time and parameter count, not an unsupported superiority claim.

## Pitfalls and checks

- Mask labels only; downstream SST-2 test labels excluded from selection.
- Never fit statistics on validation labels or mix held-out examples into training.
- Check shape and finite outputs before interpreting quality; an unrelated baseline cannot satisfy the intended mechanism.
- Draft targets and safety limits have not been GPU-calibrated. No pass threshold is advertised.
