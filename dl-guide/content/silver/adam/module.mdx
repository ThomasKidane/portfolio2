# Adam and AdamW from scratch

## Historical question and bottleneck

[Adam: A Method for Stochastic Optimization](https://arxiv.org/abs/1412.6980) (Kingma and Ba, 2014) is the primary reference. The original question motivating this module is how to implement biased moments bias correction epsilon placement and decoupled AdamW decay; match torch updates. The practical bottleneck for this exercise is explicit: Match torch Adam and AdamW step by step including bias correction epsilon and weight decay. This is a reduced teaching adaptation, not a claim that the paper used our dataset or compute budget.

## Mechanism and equations

`m=beta1*m+(1-beta1)*g; v=beta2*v+(1-beta2)*g^2; w-=eta*m_hat/(sqrt(v_hat)+epsilon).`

Derive each tensor's dimensions before implementing the equation. State the normalization axis, numerical stabilizer and train/evaluation behavior. Distinguish a mathematical mechanism from the full original training system.
