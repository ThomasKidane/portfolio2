# Knowledge-distillation golf

## Historical question and bottleneck

[Distilling the Knowledge in a Neural Network](https://arxiv.org/abs/1503.02531) (Hinton et al., 2015) is the primary reference. The original question motivating this module is how to compress a frozen teacher using temperature-scaled KL and a small student parameter cap. The practical bottleneck for this exercise is explicit: Frozen teacher temperature-squared KL scaling; quality versus student parameter count. This is a reduced teaching adaptation, not a claim that the paper used our dataset or compute budget.

## Mechanism and equations

`L=(1-lambda)*CE(student,y)+lambda*T^2*KL(softmax(teacher/T)||softmax(student/T)).`

Derive each tensor's dimensions before implementing the equation. State the normalization axis, numerical stabilizer and train/evaluation behavior. Distinguish a mathematical mechanism from the full original training system.

## Implementation notes

Input/task: **cifar10**. Output: **Image -> student logits**. Optimize or report **accuracy** in the **maximize** direction. Frozen teacher temperature-squared KL scaling; quality versus student parameter count.

This is a draft skeleton: reference training and task-specific tests are not implemented. The explicit stub must not be mistaken for a finished model.

## Controlled experiment

Compare hard labels alone with soft-target distillation at equal student compute. Keep split, seed, optimizer schedule and training budget fixed unless that factor is the experimental variable. Report raw quality, wall time and parameter count, not an unsupported superiority claim.

## Pitfalls and checks

- Frozen teacher temperature-squared KL scaling; quality versus student parameter count.
- Never fit statistics on validation labels or mix held-out examples into training.
- Check shape and finite outputs before interpreting quality; an unrelated baseline cannot satisfy the intended mechanism.
- Draft targets and safety limits have not been GPU-calibrated. No pass threshold is advertised.
