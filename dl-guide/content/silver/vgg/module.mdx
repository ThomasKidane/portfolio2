# VGG 3x3 convolution golf

## Historical question and bottleneck

[Very Deep Convolutional Networks for Large-Scale Image Recognition](https://arxiv.org/abs/1409.1556) (Simonyan and Zisserman, 2014) is the primary reference. The original question motivating this module is how to stack 3x3 convolution blocks and minimize parameters at fixed CIFAR-10 quality. The practical bottleneck for this exercise is explicit: Parameter golf at a declared quality target using 3x3 stacks. This is a reduced teaching adaptation, not a claim that the paper used our dataset or compute budget.

## Mechanism and equations

$$
\begin{aligned}
h_1=\operatorname{ReLU}(W_1*x+b_1),\quad h_2=\operatorname{ReLU}(W_2*h_1+b_2),\\ R_2=1+(3-1)+(3-1)=5
\end{aligned}
$$

Here both W kernels are three by three, the star is convolution, b are biases, and R is receptive-field side length. With unit stride and dilation and no intervening pooling, two such layers see five by five inputs while retaining an intermediate nonlinearity.

Derive each tensor's dimensions before implementing the equation. State the normalization axis, numerical stabilizer and train/evaluation behavior. Distinguish a mathematical mechanism from the full original training system.

## Implementation notes

Input/task: **cifar10**. Output: **[N,3,32,32] -> [N,10] logits**. Optimize or report **accuracy** in the **maximize** direction. Parameter golf at a declared quality target using 3x3 stacks.

This is a draft skeleton: reference training and task-specific tests are not implemented. The explicit stub must not be mistaken for a finished model.

## Controlled experiment

Replace two 3x3 convolutions with one 5x5 layer at matched output width. Keep split, seed, optimizer schedule and training budget fixed unless that factor is the experimental variable. Report raw quality, wall time and parameter count, not an unsupported superiority claim.

## Pitfalls and checks

- Parameter golf at a declared quality target using 3x3 stacks.
- Never fit statistics on validation labels or mix held-out examples into training.
- Check shape and finite outputs before interpreting quality; an unrelated baseline cannot satisfy the intended mechanism.
- Draft targets and safety limits have not been GPU-calibrated. No pass threshold is advertised.
