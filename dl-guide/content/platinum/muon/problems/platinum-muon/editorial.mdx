# Muon versus AdamW: derivation and implementation notes

## The mechanism

$$
\begin{aligned}
M_t=\beta M_{t-1}+G_t,\quad M_t=U\Sigma V^\top,\\ O_t=UV^\top,\quad W_{t+1}=W_t-\eta O_t
\end{aligned}
$$

Here W is a matrix weight, G its gradient, M momentum, beta its coefficient, and eta the step size. U/Sigma/V denote a compact SVD; O is the ideal polar-factor direction. Practical Muon approximates this with finite Newton-Schulz iterations and may use Nesterov, shape scaling, and separate vector-parameter updates; this is a simplified core, not an exact implementation prescription.
