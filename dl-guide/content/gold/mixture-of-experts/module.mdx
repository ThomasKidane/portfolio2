# Switch mixture of experts

## Historical question and bottleneck

[Switch Transformers: Scaling to Trillion Parameter Models with Simple and Efficient Sparsity](https://arxiv.org/abs/2101.03961) (Fedus et al., 2021) is the primary reference. The original question motivating this module is how to implement top-1 routing capacity handling and load-balancing loss; compare iso-FLOP dense baselines. The practical bottleneck for this exercise is explicit: Capacity overflow deterministic; report active FLOPs and expert balance. This is a reduced teaching adaptation, not a claim that the paper used our dataset or compute budget.

## Mechanism and equations

`y=sum_e gate_e(x)*expert_e(x), with top-1 routing and explicit capacity; record auxiliary load balancing.`

Derive each tensor's dimensions before implementing the equation. State the normalization axis, numerical stabilizer and train/evaluation behavior. Distinguish a mathematical mechanism from the full original training system.

## Implementation notes

Input/task: **tinyshakespeare**. Output: **[N,T] tokens -> [N,T,V] logits plus routing statistics**. Optimize or report **bpc** in the **minimize** direction. Capacity overflow deterministic; report active FLOPs and expert balance.

This is a draft skeleton: reference training and task-specific tests are not implemented. The explicit stub must not be mistaken for a finished model.

## Controlled experiment

Remove balancing loss and measure expert-load collapse. Keep split, seed, optimizer schedule and training budget fixed unless that factor is the experimental variable. Report raw quality, wall time and parameter count, not an unsupported superiority claim.
