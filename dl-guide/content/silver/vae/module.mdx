# Variational autoencoder

## Historical question and bottleneck

[Auto-Encoding Variational Bayes](https://arxiv.org/abs/1312.6114) (Kingma and Welling, 2013) is the primary reference. The original question motivating this module is how to implement diagonal-Gaussian reparameterization and MNIST negative ELBO with explicit reconstruction and KL terms. The practical bottleneck for this exercise is explicit: State Bernoulli reconstruction likelihood and summed KL convention. This is a reduced teaching adaptation, not a claim that the paper used our dataset or compute budget.

## Mechanism and equations

$$
\begin{aligned}
-\mathrm{ELBO}=\mathbb E_{q_\phi(z\mid x)}[-\log p_\theta(x\mid z)]+D_{\mathrm{KL}}(q_\phi(z\mid x)\Vert p(z)),\\ z=\mu_\phi(x)+\sigma_\phi(x)\odot\varepsilon,\quad\varepsilon\sim\mathcal N(0,I)
\end{aligned}
$$

Here x is data, z a latent, q the encoder posterior, p the prior/decoder, theta and phi their parameters, and mu/sigma the diagonal Gaussian mean/standard deviation. KL is relative entropy; reconstruction loss must match the decoder likelihood.

Derive each tensor's dimensions before implementing the equation. State the normalization axis, numerical stabilizer and train/evaluation behavior. Distinguish a mathematical mechanism from the full original training system.

## Implementation notes

Input/task: **mnist**. Output: **Image -> reconstruction logits posterior mean and log variance**. Optimize or report **negative_elbo** in the **minimize** direction. State Bernoulli reconstruction likelihood and summed KL convention.

This is a draft skeleton: reference training and task-specific tests are not implemented. The explicit stub must not be mistaken for a finished model.

## Controlled experiment

Set the KL weight to zero and inspect posterior collapse and sample quality. Keep split, seed, optimizer schedule and training budget fixed unless that factor is the experimental variable. Report raw quality, wall time and parameter count, not an unsupported superiority claim.

## Pitfalls and checks

- State Bernoulli reconstruction likelihood and summed KL convention.
- Never fit statistics on validation labels or mix held-out examples into training.
- Check shape and finite outputs before interpreting quality; an unrelated baseline cannot satisfy the intended mechanism.
