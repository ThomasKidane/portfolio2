# Dropout and weight decay: derivation and implementation notes

## The mechanism

$$
\begin{aligned}
L_{\mathrm{total}}=L_{\mathrm{data}}+\frac\lambda2\lVert w\rVert_2^2,\\ w_{t+1}=(1-\eta\lambda)w_t-\eta u_t
\end{aligned}
$$

Here w is the weight vector, lambda the penalty/decay coefficient, eta the learning rate, and u the optimizer's data-gradient update direction. The first line is coupled L2 regularization; the second is decoupled decay. They agree for plain SGD but generally not adaptive optimizers.
