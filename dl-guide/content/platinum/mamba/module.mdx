# State-space sequence models

## Historical question and bottleneck

[Mamba: Linear-Time Sequence Modeling with Selective State Spaces](https://arxiv.org/abs/2312.00752) (Gu and Dao, 2023) is the primary reference. The original question motivating this module is how to compare S4-style recurrence and input-dependent selective state spaces on variable-length copying. The practical bottleneck for this exercise is explicit: Compare recurrence and parallel scan numerically; test longer unseen sequences. This is a reduced teaching adaptation, not a claim that the paper used our dataset or compute budget.

## Mechanism and equations

`state_t=A_t*state_prev+B_t*x_t; y_t=C_t*state_t; selective coefficients depend on inputs.`

Derive each tensor's dimensions before implementing the equation. State the normalization axis, numerical stabilizer and train/evaluation behavior. Distinguish a mathematical mechanism from the full original training system.

## Implementation notes

Input/task: **generated-copying**. Output: **[N,T] symbols -> [N,T,V] logits**. Optimize or report **sequence_accuracy** in the **maximize** direction. Compare recurrence and parallel scan numerically; test longer unseen sequences.

This is a draft skeleton: reference training and task-specific tests are not implemented. The explicit stub must not be mistaken for a finished model.

## Controlled experiment

Remove input-dependent selection while matching state dimension. Keep split, seed, optimizer schedule and training budget fixed unless that factor is the experimental variable. Report raw quality, wall time and parameter count, not an unsupported superiority claim.

## Pitfalls and checks

- Compare recurrence and parallel scan numerically; test longer unseen sequences.
- Never fit statistics on validation labels or mix held-out examples into training.
- Check shape and finite outputs before interpreting quality; an unrelated baseline cannot satisfy the intended mechanism.
