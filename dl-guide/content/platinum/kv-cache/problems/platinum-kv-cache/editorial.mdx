# KV cache and speculative decoding: derivation and implementation notes

## The mechanism

$$
\begin{aligned}
K_{\mathrm{cache}}=[K_{\mathrm{past}};K_{\mathrm{new}}],\quad V_{\mathrm{cache}}=[V_{\mathrm{past}};V_{\mathrm{new}}],\\ a(x)=\min\!\left(1,\frac{p(x)}{q(x)}\right),\quad p_{\mathrm{reject}}(x)=\frac{[p(x)-q(x)]_+}{\sum_y[p(y)-q(y)]_+}
\end{aligned}
$$

Here K/V concatenate keys/values along time. In speculative sampling p is the target and q the draft distribution at the same prefix, and a accepts a token drawn from q. After rejection sample the positive-part residual; acceptance ratios alone are not the full algorithm. The residual is needed only when rejection is possible.
