# Flow matching and rectified flow DiT

## Historical question and bottleneck

[Flow Matching for Generative Modeling](https://arxiv.org/abs/2210.02747) (Lipman et al., 2022) is the primary reference. The original question motivating this module is how to fit conditional velocity fields with a small diffusion-transformer backbone and integrate samples. The practical bottleneck for this exercise is explicit: Time endpoint conventions and ODE solver direction; DiT is a separate architectural reference. This is a reduced teaching adaptation, not a claim that the paper used our dataset or compute budget.

## Mechanism and equations

$$
\begin{aligned}
x_t=(1-t)x_0+tx_1,\quad t\sim\mathcal U(0,1),\\ L=\mathbb E\lVert v_\theta(x_t,t)-(x_1-x_0)\rVert_2^2
\end{aligned}
$$

Here x-zero is noise, x-one data drawn under a declared coupling, and v-theta the learned velocity. This conditional flow-matching loss uses a straight-line interpolation; generation integrates the velocity from time zero to one.

Derive each tensor's dimensions before implementing the equation. State the normalization axis, numerical stabilizer and train/evaluation behavior. Distinguish a mathematical mechanism from the full original training system.
