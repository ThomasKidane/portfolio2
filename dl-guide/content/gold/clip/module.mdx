# CLIP paired-retrieval capstone

## Historical question and bottleneck

[Learning Transferable Visual Models From Natural Language Supervision](https://arxiv.org/abs/2103.00020) (Radford et al., 2021) is the primary reference. The original question motivating this module is how to align licensed image-caption pairs using symmetric contrastive loss and report retrieval recall; explicit Gold capstone exception. The practical bottleneck for this exercise is explicit: Gold capstone exception; evaluate both retrieval directions on held-out pairs. This is a reduced teaching adaptation, not a claim that the paper used our dataset or compute budget.

## Mechanism and equations

$$
\begin{aligned}
S_{ij}=\frac{u_i^\top v_j}{\tau},\\ L=-\frac1{2B}\sum_{i=1}^B\left[\log\frac{e^{S_{ii}}}{\sum_j e^{S_{ij}}}+\log\frac{e^{S_{ii}}}{\sum_j e^{S_{ji}}}\right]
\end{aligned}
$$

Here u and v are unit-normalized image/text embeddings for B aligned pairs, S similarity logits, and tau positive temperature. The two terms classify image-to-text and text-to-image pairs respectively.

Derive each tensor's dimensions before implementing the equation. State the normalization axis, numerical stabilizer and train/evaluation behavior. Distinguish a mathematical mechanism from the full original training system.

## Implementation notes

Input/task: **licensed-image-caption-pairs**. Output: **Image and text -> normalized paired embeddings**. Optimize or report **retrieval_recall_at_1** in the **maximize** direction. Gold capstone exception; evaluate both retrieval directions on held-out pairs.

This is a draft skeleton: reference training and task-specific tests are not implemented. The explicit stub must not be mistaken for a finished model.

## Controlled experiment

Compare one-directional and symmetric contrastive losses. Keep split, seed, optimizer schedule and training budget fixed unless that factor is the experimental variable. Report raw quality, wall time and parameter count, not an unsupported superiority claim.
