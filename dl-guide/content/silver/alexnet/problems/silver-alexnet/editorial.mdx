## Task-specific evaluation

Dataset: **cifar10**. Model contract: **[N,3,32,32] -> [N,10] logits**. Metric: **accuracy**, direction **maximize**. CIFAR adaptation not ImageNet reproduction.

Requested goal: 0.85. This is aspirational, not a verified or calibrated passing threshold.



## Required ablation

Remove dropout while preserving the training schedule. Keep the seed, split, training steps and optimizer fixed. Report both quality and compute cost; one run is not statistical evidence.

## Failure modes

- Fitting on validation labels or allowing split overlap invalidates evaluation.
- Matching output shape alone does not establish that the intended mechanism was implemented.
- A small teaching model is not the original full-scale experiment.
