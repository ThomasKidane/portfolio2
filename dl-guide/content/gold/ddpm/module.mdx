# Denoising diffusion probabilistic model

## Historical question and bottleneck

[Denoising Diffusion Probabilistic Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2006.11239) (Ho et al., 2020) is the primary reference. The original question motivating this module is how to implement forward noise schedules and epsilon prediction on MNIST with a CIFAR extension. The practical bottleneck for this exercise is explicit: Schedule buffers and reverse variance correct; CIFAR extension requires separate calibration. This is a reduced teaching adaptation, not a claim that the paper used our dataset or compute budget.

## Mechanism and equations

`x_t=sqrt(alpha_bar_t)*x_0+sqrt(1-alpha_bar_t)*epsilon; L=mean((epsilon_model-epsilon)^2).`

Derive each tensor's dimensions before implementing the equation. State the normalization axis, numerical stabilizer and train/evaluation behavior. Distinguish a mathematical mechanism from the full original training system.

## Implementation notes

Input/task: **mnist**. Output: **Noisy image timestep -> predicted noise**. Optimize or report **denoising_mse** in the **minimize** direction. Schedule buffers and reverse variance correct; CIFAR extension requires separate calibration.

This is a draft skeleton: reference training and task-specific tests are not implemented. The explicit stub must not be mistaken for a finished model.

## Controlled experiment

Compare linear and cosine schedules at equal denoiser evaluations. Keep split, seed, optimizer schedule and training budget fixed unless that factor is the experimental variable. Report raw quality, wall time and parameter count, not an unsupported superiority claim.

## Pitfalls and checks

- Schedule buffers and reverse variance correct; CIFAR extension requires separate calibration.
- Never fit statistics on validation labels or mix held-out examples into training.
- Check shape and finite outputs before interpreting quality; an unrelated baseline cannot satisfy the intended mechanism.
