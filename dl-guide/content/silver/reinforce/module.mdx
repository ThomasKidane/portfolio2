# REINFORCE with a baseline

## Historical question and bottleneck

[Simple statistical gradient-following algorithms for connectionist reinforcement learning](https://doi.org/10.1007/BF00992696) (Williams, 1992) is the primary reference. The original question motivating this module is how to estimate CartPole policy gradients with reward-to-go and a learned value baseline. The practical bottleneck for this exercise is explicit: Detach advantage from policy gradient; no evaluation-episode training. This is a reduced teaching adaptation, not a claim that the paper used our dataset or compute budget.

## Mechanism and equations

$$
\begin{aligned}
\nabla_\theta J=\mathbb E\!\left[\sum_t\nabla_\theta\log\pi_\theta(a_t\mid s_t)(G_t-b(s_t))\right],\\ G_t=\sum_{k=t}^{T-1}\gamma^{k-t}r_k
\end{aligned}
$$

Here pi is the policy, s/a states/actions, G reward-to-go, b an action-independent baseline, r reward, and T episode length. This objective uses undiscounted episodic returns when gamma equals one; a start-state discounted objective additionally weights each time term by gamma to the power t. Do not backpropagate through the sampled advantage in the policy update.

Derive each tensor's dimensions before implementing the equation. State the normalization axis, numerical stabilizer and train/evaluation behavior. Distinguish a mathematical mechanism from the full original training system.

## Implementation notes

Input/task: **cartpole**. Output: **Observation -> action logits plus baseline**. Optimize or report **episode_return** in the **maximize** direction. Detach advantage from policy gradient; no evaluation-episode training.

This is a draft skeleton: reference training and task-specific tests are not implemented. The explicit stub must not be mistaken for a finished model.

## Controlled experiment

Remove the baseline and compare gradient variance across seeds. Keep split, seed, optimizer schedule and training budget fixed unless that factor is the experimental variable. Report raw quality, wall time and parameter count, not an unsupported superiority claim.

## Pitfalls and checks

- Detach advantage from policy gradient; no evaluation-episode training.
- Never fit statistics on validation labels or mix held-out examples into training.
- Check shape and finite outputs before interpreting quality; an unrelated baseline cannot satisfy the intended mechanism.
- Draft targets and safety limits have not been GPU-calibrated. No pass threshold is advertised.
