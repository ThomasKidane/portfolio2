## Task-specific evaluation

Dataset: **mnist**. Model contract: **Image -> reconstruction logits posterior mean and log variance**. Metric: **negative_elbo**, direction **minimize**. State Bernoulli reconstruction likelihood and summed KL convention.

## Required ablation

Set the KL weight to zero and inspect posterior collapse and sample quality. Keep the seed, split, training steps and optimizer fixed. Report both quality and compute cost; one run is not statistical evidence.

## Failure modes

- Fitting on validation labels or allowing split overlap invalidates evaluation.
- Matching output shape alone does not establish that the intended mechanism was implemented.
- A small teaching model is not the original full-scale experiment.
