# INT8 and four-bit quantization: derivation and implementation notes

## The mechanism

$$
\begin{aligned}
q=\operatorname{clip}\!\left(\operatorname{round}(x/s)+z,q_{\min},q_{\max}\right),\\ \hat x=s(q-z)
\end{aligned}
$$

Here x is a real value, s a positive scale, z an integer zero point, q-min/q-max integer limits, and hat-x dequantized reconstruction. Declare per-tensor or per-channel scale and the rounding convention.
