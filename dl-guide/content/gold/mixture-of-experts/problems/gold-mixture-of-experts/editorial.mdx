# Switch mixture of experts: derivation and implementation notes

## The mechanism

$$
\begin{aligned}
p(x)=\operatorname{softmax}(g(x)),\quad e^*=\arg\max_e p_e(x),\\ y=p_{e^*}(x)f_{e^*}(x),\quad L_{\mathrm{aux}}=E\sum_{e=1}^E f_e^{\mathrm{load}}\bar p_e
\end{aligned}
$$

Here g is router logits, f-e expert networks, E expert count, load-f the batch fraction assigned to an expert, and bar-p its mean routing probability. This is a Switch-style top-one convention; specify capacity and overflow handling separately and weight the auxiliary loss explicitly.
