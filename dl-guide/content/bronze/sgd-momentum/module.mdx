# SGD with momentum

## Historical question and bottleneck

[Some methods of speeding up the convergence of iteration methods](https://doi.org/10.1016/0041-5553(64)90137-5) (Polyak, 1964) is the primary reference. The original question motivating this module is how to implement velocity updates on an ill-conditioned quadratic and a tiny MLP. The practical bottleneck for this exercise is explicit: Explicit v=beta*v+grad and w-=lr*v; record quadratic objective for beta zero and nonzero. This is a reduced teaching adaptation, not a claim that the paper used our dataset or compute budget.

## Mechanism and equations

$$
\begin{aligned}
g_t=\nabla L(w_t),\quad v_t=\beta v_{t-1}+g_t,\\ w_{t+1}=w_t-\eta v_t
\end{aligned}
$$

Here L is the loss, w the weights, g the gradient, v the velocity initialized to zero, beta the momentum coefficient, and eta the learning rate. This convention does not multiply the new gradient by one minus beta.

Derive each tensor's dimensions before implementing the equation. State the normalization axis, numerical stabilizer and train/evaluation behavior. Distinguish a mathematical mechanism from the full original training system.

## Implementation notes

Input/task: **synthetic-classification**. Output: **[N,D] -> [N,C] logits**. Optimize or report **accuracy** in the **maximize** direction. Explicit v=beta*v+grad and w-=lr*v; record quadratic objective for beta zero and nonzero.

An executable bounded reference and mechanism tests are available; work in the starter before opening the editorial.

## Controlled experiment

Set momentum to zero at the same learning rate and compare objective traces. Keep split, seed, optimizer schedule and training budget fixed unless that factor is the experimental variable. Report raw quality, wall time and parameter count, not an unsupported superiority claim.
