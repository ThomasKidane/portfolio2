# Sparse autoencoders: derivation and implementation notes

## The mechanism

$$
\begin{aligned}
z=\operatorname{ReLU}(W_e(x-b_d)+b_e),\quad\hat x=W_dz+b_d,\\ L=\lVert\hat x-x\rVert_2^2+\lambda\lVert z\rVert_1
\end{aligned}
$$

Here x is a frozen-model activation, z sparse features, W-e/W-d encoder/decoder matrices, b biases, and lambda the sparsity penalty. Average over samples and constrain decoder column norms to prevent trivial rescaling of the L1 penalty.
