# Modern transformer components

## Historical question and bottleneck

[Llama 2: Open Foundation and Fine-Tuned Chat Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2307.09288) (Touvron et al., 2023) is the primary reference. The original question motivating this module is how to combine RoPE RMSNorm SwiGLU and grouped-query attention with shape and parameter audits. The practical bottleneck for this exercise is explicit: RoPE even pairs RMSNorm epsilon SwiGLU width and GQA head sharing. This is a reduced teaching adaptation, not a claim that the paper used our dataset or compute budget.

## Mechanism and equations

$$
\begin{aligned}
\operatorname{RMSNorm}(x)=g\odot\frac{x}{\sqrt{\frac1d\sum_{i=1}^d x_i^2+\epsilon}},\\ \operatorname{SwiGLU}(x)=[\operatorname{swish}(xW_g)\odot(xW_v)]W_o,\\ \operatorname{swish}(u)=u\sigma(u)
\end{aligned}
$$

Here d is feature width, g learned gain, epsilon a stabilizer, W-g/W-v/W-o gate/value/output projections, and sigma sigmoid. RMS normalization does not subtract a mean; the SwiGLU expression uses row-vector inputs and omits biases.

Derive each tensor's dimensions before implementing the equation. State the normalization axis, numerical stabilizer and train/evaluation behavior. Distinguish a mathematical mechanism from the full original training system.

## Implementation notes

Input/task: **tinyshakespeare**. Output: **[N,T] tokens -> [N,T,V] logits**. Optimize or report **bpc** in the **minimize** direction. RoPE even pairs RMSNorm epsilon SwiGLU width and GQA head sharing.

This is a draft skeleton: reference training and task-specific tests are not implemented. The explicit stub must not be mistaken for a finished model.

## Controlled experiment

Replace one component at a time using matched token and parameter budgets. Keep split, seed, optimizer schedule and training budget fixed unless that factor is the experimental variable. Report raw quality, wall time and parameter count, not an unsupported superiority claim.

## Pitfalls and checks

- RoPE even pairs RMSNorm epsilon SwiGLU width and GQA head sharing.
- Never fit statistics on validation labels or mix held-out examples into training.
- Check shape and finite outputs before interpreting quality; an unrelated baseline cannot satisfy the intended mechanism.
