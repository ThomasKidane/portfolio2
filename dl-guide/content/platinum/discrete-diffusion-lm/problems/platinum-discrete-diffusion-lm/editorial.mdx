# Absorbing discrete diffusion language model: derivation and implementation notes

## The mechanism

$$
\begin{aligned}
q(x_t\mid x_{t-1})=\operatorname{Cat}(x_{t-1}Q_t),\\ q(x_t\mid x_0)=\operatorname{Cat}(x_0\bar Q_t),\quad\bar Q_t=Q_1\cdots Q_t,\\ (Q_t)_{mm}=1
\end{aligned}
$$

Here x is a row one-hot token, Q a row-stochastic transition matrix, Cat a categorical distribution, and m an absorbing mask state. Once masked the forward process stays masked; a learned reverse process reconstructs tokens.
