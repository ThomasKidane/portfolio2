# Seq2Seq with attention

## Historical question and bottleneck

[Neural Machine Translation by Jointly Learning to Align and Translate](https://arxiv.org/abs/1409.0473) (Bahdanau et al., 2014) is the primary reference. The original question motivating this module is how to translate deterministic generated date strings with encoder-decoder additive attention; aspirational exact match 0.95. The practical bottleneck for this exercise is explicit: Split unique underlying dates before formatting; exact full-string match. This is a reduced teaching adaptation, not a claim that the paper used our dataset or compute budget.

## Mechanism and equations

`alpha_t=softmax(score(s_prev,h_i)); context_t=sum_i alpha_ti*h_i.`

Derive each tensor's dimensions before implementing the equation. State the normalization axis, numerical stabilizer and train/evaluation behavior. Distinguish a mathematical mechanism from the full original training system.

## Implementation notes

Input/task: **generated-dates**. Output: **Date-token sequences -> decoded date-token sequences**. Optimize or report **exact_match** in the **maximize** direction. Split unique underlying dates before formatting; exact full-string match.

This is a draft skeleton: reference training and task-specific tests are not implemented. The explicit stub must not be mistaken for a finished model.

## Controlled experiment

Remove attention while keeping encoder decoder and date splits fixed. Keep split, seed, optimizer schedule and training budget fixed unless that factor is the experimental variable. Report raw quality, wall time and parameter count, not an unsupported superiority claim.

## Pitfalls and checks

- Split unique underlying dates before formatting; exact full-string match.
- Never fit statistics on validation labels or mix held-out examples into training.
- Check shape and finite outputs before interpreting quality; an unrelated baseline cannot satisfy the intended mechanism.
