# Neural probabilistic language model

## Historical question and bottleneck

[A Neural Probabilistic Language Model](https://www.jmlr.org/papers/v3/bengio03a.html) (Bengio et al., 2003) is the primary reference. The original question motivating this module is how to learn fixed-context embeddings and predict the next TinyShakespeare character; compare bits per character with a smoothed n-gram. The practical bottleneck for this exercise is explicit: Character-level natural-log cross-entropy divided by log(2); compare a training-only smoothed n-gram. This is a reduced teaching adaptation, not a claim that the paper used our dataset or compute budget.

## Mechanism and equations

`p(c_next|context)=softmax(W_out*tanh(W_h*concat(emb(context))+b_h)+b_out); bpc=CE/log(2).`

Derive each tensor's dimensions before implementing the equation. State the normalization axis, numerical stabilizer and train/evaluation behavior. Distinguish a mathematical mechanism from the full original training system.

## Implementation notes

Input/task: **tinyshakespeare**. Output: **[N,context] token IDs -> [N,vocab] next-character logits**. Optimize or report **bpc** in the **minimize** direction. Character-level natural-log cross-entropy divided by log(2); compare a training-only smoothed n-gram.

An executable bounded reference and mechanism tests are available; work in the starter before opening the editorial.

## Controlled experiment

Remove learned embeddings or change context length while keeping the corpus split fixed. Keep split, seed, optimizer schedule and training budget fixed unless that factor is the experimental variable. Report raw quality, wall time and parameter count, not an unsupported superiority claim.

## Pitfalls and checks

- Character-level natural-log cross-entropy divided by log(2); compare a training-only smoothed n-gram.
- Never fit statistics on validation labels or mix held-out examples into training.
- Check shape and finite outputs before interpreting quality; an unrelated baseline cannot satisfy the intended mechanism.
- Draft targets and safety limits have not been GPU-calibrated. No pass threshold is advertised.
