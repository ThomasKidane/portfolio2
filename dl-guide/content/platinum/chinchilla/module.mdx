# Compute-optimal language modeling

## Historical question and bottleneck

[Training Compute-Optimal Large Language Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2203.15556) (Hoffmann et al., 2022) is the primary reference. The original question motivating this module is how to allocate tokens and parameters across fixed-compute runs and fit a compute-optimal frontier. The practical bottleneck for this exercise is explicit: Compare allocations at equal measured training FLOPs. This is a reduced teaching adaptation, not a claim that the paper used our dataset or compute budget.

## Mechanism and equations

$$
\begin{aligned}
C\approx\kappa ND,\\ (N^*,D^*)=\arg\min_{N,D>0}\left[L_\infty+AN^{-\alpha}+BD^{-\beta}\right]\quad\text{subject to }\kappa ND\le C_0
\end{aligned}
$$

Here N is parameters, D training tokens, kappa an architecture-dependent compute factor, C-zero a fixed budget, and the other constants fit loss observations. This approximate training-compute model does not replace measured hardware time or prescribe universal optimal counts.

Derive each tensor's dimensions before implementing the equation. State the normalization axis, numerical stabilizer and train/evaluation behavior. Distinguish a mathematical mechanism from the full original training system.

## Implementation notes

Input/task: **measured-training-runs**. Output: **Compute allocation -> fitted loss frontier**. Optimize or report **heldout_loss** in the **minimize** direction. Compare allocations at equal measured training FLOPs.

This is a draft skeleton: reference training and task-specific tests are not implemented. The explicit stub must not be mistaken for a finished model.

## Controlled experiment

Compare equal-parameter with equal-compute allocations. Keep split, seed, optimizer schedule and training budget fixed unless that factor is the experimental variable. Report raw quality, wall time and parameter count, not an unsupported superiority claim.

## Pitfalls and checks

- Compare allocations at equal measured training FLOPs.
- Never fit statistics on validation labels or mix held-out examples into training.
- Check shape and finite outputs before interpreting quality; an unrelated baseline cannot satisfy the intended mechanism.
- Draft targets and safety limits have not been GPU-calibrated. No pass threshold is advertised.
