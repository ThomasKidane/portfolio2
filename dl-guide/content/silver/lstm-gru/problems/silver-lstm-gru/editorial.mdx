# LSTM and GRU: derivation and implementation notes

## The mechanism

$$
\begin{aligned}
(i_t,f_t,o_t)=\sigma(W_g[x_t;h_{t-1}]+b_g),\\ \tilde c_t=\tanh(W_c[x_t;h_{t-1}]+b_c),\\ c_t=f_t\odot c_{t-1}+i_t\odot\tilde c_t,\quad h_t=o_t\odot\tanh(c_t),\\ h_t^{\mathrm{GRU}}=(1-z_t)\odot h_{t-1}+z_t\odot\tilde h_t
\end{aligned}
$$

Here x is input, h hidden state, c LSTM cell state, i/f/o input/forget/output gates, and W/b learned affine maps; brackets concatenate. In the shown GRU convention z weights the candidate, whose computation includes a reset gate. Products are elementwise.
